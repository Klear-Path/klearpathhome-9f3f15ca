Lost your ID or birth certificate?
In Pennsylvania, replacing them is free.

You just need an organization to sign for you — and that is the only reason most people never get them. That's the gap we close, and it costs you nothing.

Get them in this order

1. Birth certificate

Proves who you are. Almost everything else needs it.

2. Social Security card

Always free, but they want proof of identity first.

3. Photo ID

PennDOT wants identity, your Social Security card, and an address — so it comes last.

Why almost nobody gets the free ones

Pennsylvania waives these fees for people experiencing homelessness. It has for years.

But the form has a second signature line, and only three people can sign it: a director of an organization where you live or get services, a social worker helping you get benefits, or a lawyer representing you.

If you don't already have one of those, the waiver may as well not exist. The money was never the real barrier. The signature was.

What we actually do

  • Work out which document you need first, so you don't lose a day on a trip that was never going to work
  • Sign the form where we qualify — or help you get the right person to sign it
  • Pay the fee ourselves when there's no waiver
  • Tell you which office to go to, what to bring, and what to say at the counter

No income check. No proof of hardship. No program to join. You keep every document. We never hold your originals.
